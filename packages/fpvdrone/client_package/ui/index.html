Calibration: if the picture rolls/pitches/yaws the wrong way compared to the drone, flip the matching axis here. These map the simulation onto JC3's camera angles and only need setting once.

Click a key to rebind it. Keyboard flight: hold W to take off/climb (Shift+W faster), S to descend, A/D yaw, arrow keys pitch & roll. The default angle mode + altitude assist holds height when you let go. F9 shows input/terrain debug info.

Swarm: call wingmen in (they take off from your launch point and join a formation around you). Dive at something and they circle above it; when your drone impacts, control jumps to the wingman best placed to see the impact, already aiming its camera at it, so you can go straight into the next attack. "Attack" sends every wingman at players, vehicles and drones near you. On a RadioMaster, set the switches you want as momentary (e.g. SH) to an output channel and use Detect on the Controls tab.
